Create a modern, attractive, mobile-first food ordering web app for a small Nigerian food business called **PB DELICACIES**.

### BUSINESS INFORMATION

* Business name: **PB DELICACIES**
* Location: **Ido-Ekiti, Ekiti State, Nigeria**
* Phone/WhatsApp: **08150781154**
* Business type:

  1. Weekend food delivery
  2. Customers can order prepared meals for delivery
  3. Customers can also book PB DELICACIES to come to their homes and cook for them, especially on weekends
* The menu changes weekly, so the owner must be able to easily update the weekly menu and food availability.

### MAIN GOAL

Build an easy-to-use ordering app where customers in and around Ido-Ekiti can:

* View this week's available meals
* See food photos, descriptions and prices
* Select meals and quantities
* Add meals to a cart
* Enter their delivery information
* Choose delivery or home cooking service
* Place an order
* Contact PB DELICACIES through WhatsApp or phone
* View their order summary and order status

### DESIGN STYLE

Create a clean, premium but friendly Nigerian food brand aesthetic.

Use:

* Warm food-inspired colors such as deep brown, cream, orange/gold and subtle green accents
* Large, appetizing food photography
* Rounded cards and buttons
* Clear typography
* Plenty of whitespace
* Modern icons
* Subtle animations and hover effects
* Mobile-first responsive design, but make it also work well on desktop

The design should feel trustworthy, homemade, delicious and professional rather than like a large fast-food corporation.

# IMPORTANT FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

Make the prototype interactive.

The following should work:

1. Clicking **Add to Cart** adds the food to the cart.
2. Cart quantity can be increased/decreased.
3. Cart total updates automatically.
4. Customers can remove items.
5. Customers can proceed from Menu → Cart → Checkout → Order Confirmation.
6. Home Cooking should have its own booking flow.
7. WhatsApp buttons should open WhatsApp using the business number **08150781154**.
8. Navigation buttons should work between pages.
9. Food availability should be visually clear.
10. The menu should be structured so that it can easily be changed every week.
11. Use Nigerian Naira (₦) for all prices.
12. Make the entire application responsive for mobile, tablet and desktop.

### IMPORTANT

Do not make the interface overly complicated. The primary users are ordinary customers who simply want to order food quickly.

Prioritize:
**Food → Menu → Cart → Checkout → Confirmation**

The final design should look like a real, launch-ready food ordering business website/app for **PB DELICACIES in Ido-Ekiti, Ekiti State, Nigeria**, not just a generic UI concept.
